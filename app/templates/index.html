Organic Recipe Cards

🌿 Enriched Jeevamrutha (జీవామృతం / जीवामृत)

Targets: Nitrogen & Soil Microorganisms

Ingredients: 10kg cow dung, 5-10L cow urine, 2kg jaggery, 2kg pulse flour, handful fertile soil.

Preparation: Mix in 200L water drum. Stir twice daily in shade for 48 hours.

Application: Apply 200L per acre via irrigation or root drenching every 21 days.

🪵 Wood Ash Potash Booster (బూడిద పొటాష్)

Targets: Potassium Deficiency & Natural Pest Repellent

Ingredients: Sifted dry hardwood ash (50kg/acre).

Preparation: Sift clean ash; dust base of crops or steep in compost tea.

Application: Apply prior to flowering to boost grain and boll weight.
