Tailored Formulation Recipe

🌿 Enriched Jeevamrutha (జీవామృతం / जीवामृत)

Targets: Nitrogen, Microbial Soil Life

Ingredients Needed: 10kg fresh cow dung, 5-10L cow urine, 2kg jaggery, 2kg pulse flour, handful fertile soil.

Preparation: Mix in 200L water drum. Stir twice daily clockwise for 48 hours in shade.

Application: Apply 200L per acre via irrigation or root drenching every 21 days.

🪵 Wood Ash Potash Booster (బూడిద పొటాష్)

Targets: Potassium Deficiency & Pest Deterrence

Ingredients Needed: Sifted dry hardwood ash (50kg/acre).

Preparation: Sift to remove unburnt chunks. Dust base or steep in compost tea.

Application: Apply prior to squaring and flowering to boost boll weight and disease resistance.
